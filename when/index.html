Notes

  • Offsets come from your browser's own time zone database, including daylight saving. Pick a date in the future and the grid uses the rules that will apply then, not the ones in force today.
  • Columns are whole hours of the column zone's wall clock. Zones offset by thirty or forty-five minutes therefore show minutes in their cells — Kolkata and the Chatham Islands are not lining up with anyone.
  • On the day a zone springs forward, one wall-clock hour does not exist. The grid shows the instant the clock actually jumps to, which is the same thing a calendar invite would do.
